a soft place for the early days of motherhood

A gentle note: Tender Days offers comfort and self-care ideas — it is not medical care, diagnosis, or treatment, and it can't replace your doctor, midwife, nurse, or a mental-health professional. If low or anxious feelings last beyond about two weeks, get worse, or you ever have thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, please reach out right away: call or text 988, or the Maternal Mental Health Hotline at 1-833-852-6262. In an emergency, call 911.
