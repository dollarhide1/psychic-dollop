Good to know

Questions, answered gently

How does the free trial work?

You get full access free for 7 days. You won't be charged today, and we'll remind you before the trial ends. If it's not for you, cancel anytime before day 7 and pay nothing. After that it's $7.99/month.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — anytime, in a couple of taps, no questions asked. You'll keep access until the end of your current period.

Is this therapy or medical care?

No. Tender Days is a wellness companion for self-care and reflection — it isn't medical care, diagnosis, or treatment, and it doesn't replace your doctor, midwife, nurse, or a mental-health professional. If low or anxious feelings linger or frighten you, please reach out to a professional. The app also surfaces free, permanent crisis lines (like 988) any time you need them.

Is my information private?

Your journal, letters, and moments stay on your own device — they're never uploaded to our servers. The only things we keep are your email address and your subscription status, so we can run your account. We don't sell data or run ad trackers, and you can delete your account anytime.

What do I need to use it?

Just a phone, tablet, or computer with a web browser. There's nothing to install, and it works beautifully one-handed for those long feeds.
